# Domains, cross-functional data, and master data

> AI-generated synthesis from Charlotte Ledoux's public writing. Not authored or endorsed by her. No paid templates are included.

Domains are the main scoping device in the corpus: they make ownership concrete, reveal overlaps, and force negotiation around shared data.

## Public-grounded takeaways
- A data domain is a logical grouping of data owned and managed by a business part.
- Mapping can be function-based, value-based, process-based, or hybrid.
- Cross-functional data includes master, reference, and golden data.
- Master data initiatives only make sense when pain, scale, integration, and readiness justify the effort.
- Shared contracts and co-ownership are the practical answer when several teams rely on the same data.

## Synthesis / inference
- The corpus treats domain mapping as a negotiation exercise, not a diagramming exercise.
- The most useful boundary is the one that clarifies who can act when the data is shared.
