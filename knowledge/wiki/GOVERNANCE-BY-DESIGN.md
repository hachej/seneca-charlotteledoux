# Governance by design: embed the rules in the flow

> AI-generated synthesis from Charlotte Ledoux's public writing. Not authored or endorsed by her. No paid templates are included.

The public material moves governance away from after-the-fact review and into creation, transformation, consumption, access, and project kickoff.

## Public-grounded takeaways
- Governance should be embedded at the moments where data is created, transformed, consumed, or requested.
- The invisible layer is the informal workarounds, tacit rules, and shadow governance already running the business.
- Policy as code turns rules into machine-readable checks instead of static documents.
- Starter kits and small-company policies should be lightweight, practical, and reusable.
- Governance belongs inside existing workflows rather than in a separate bureaucracy.

## Synthesis / inference
- This is the clearest design move in the corpus: make the good path the easy path.
- Charlotte's public examples consistently move from documents to operating habits.
