# Foundations: what governance is for

> AI-generated synthesis from Charlotte Ledoux's public writing. Not authored or endorsed by her. No paid templates are included.

Charlotte repeatedly starts from plain-language framing: define the rules of the game, promote data use, and limit risk before anything else.

## Public-grounded takeaways
- Data governance is framed as a practical frame for roles, policies, and processes.
- The business/IT split matters, but governance is the bridge rather than the duplicate of management.
- Start from the highest pain point and keep the first scope small and visible.
- The recurring myths are that governance is only for big firms, only IT, a one-time project, or an expensive tool problem.
- Small companies can still govern data with lightweight rules, a few owners, and simple defaults.

## Synthesis / inference
- Across the corpus, the first move is always the same: make the problem visible and make the scope bounded.
- The tone is intentionally non-jargon so that governance can be explained to non-specialists without losing rigor.
