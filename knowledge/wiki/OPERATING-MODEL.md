# Operating model: owners, stewards, and structure

> AI-generated synthesis from Charlotte Ledoux's public writing. Not authored or endorsed by her. No paid templates are included.

This page collects the public material on roles, team shape, onboarding, and the limits of committee-based governance.

## Public-grounded takeaways
- Data Owners are accountable business leaders; Data Stewards are the operational experts who keep definitions and issues moving.
- The Data Governance Manager is a hybrid cross-functional role, not a pure technical or pure business job.
- The team model often moves from central to hybrid to federated rather than jumping straight to a large permanent team.
- Committees are only useful when they solve a real cross-domain issue or establish a launch baseline.
- Steward onboarding needs role clarity, HR framing, a toolkit, and practical enablement.

## Synthesis / inference
- The recurring pattern is not more hierarchy; it is clearer accountability with fewer ceremonial meetings.
- The most durable model reuses existing people and upgrades their operating context.
