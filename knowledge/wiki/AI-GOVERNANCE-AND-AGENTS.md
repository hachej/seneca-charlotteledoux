# AI governance and agents

> AI-generated synthesis from Charlotte Ledoux's public writing. Not authored or endorsed by her. No paid templates are included.

The AI material starts with shadow AI, then moves to inventories, guardrails, and human review before talking about agents.

## Public-grounded takeaways
- Shadow AI is the urgent starting point because it is already happening invisibly.
- First moves are practical: inventory, request form, departmental referent, acceptable-use guide.
- AI governance extends data governance into drift, explainability, versioning, and model/agent behavior.
- High-risk decisions still require human-in-the-loop review with real authority.
- By 2026 the public writing treats AI governance as a direct extension of data governance, not a separate universe.

## Synthesis / inference
- The pattern is visibility first, then control, then context.
- The corpus does not jump straight to autonomous agents; it keeps people in the loop.
