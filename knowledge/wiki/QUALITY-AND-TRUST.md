# Quality and trust: decision-ready data

> AI-generated synthesis from Charlotte Ledoux's public writing. Not authored or endorsed by her. No paid templates are included.

Quality is presented as trust, not as a generic score: if the number cannot support a decision, it is not yet governed enough.

## Public-grounded takeaways
- Focus on the priority data products and the decisions they support.
- Measure freshness, completeness, and change stability rather than abstract quality grades.
- Route incidents to the right owner, log them with context, and treat them like bugs.
- Mature quality shifts from detection to governed control at the point of use.
- It is reasonable to refuse to build until acceptance criteria and an enforcement path exist.

## Synthesis / inference
- The quality story evolves from dashboards to ownership to control.
- The public examples show business value only when quality is connected to response, not just monitoring.
