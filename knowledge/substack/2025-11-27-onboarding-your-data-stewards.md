## Framing the role

Say you did a good job (well done!) :

You have the buy-in of executives and sponsors. You have mapped the domains and owners. These Data Owners have identified Data Stewards.

It is a really good start, for sure. However, you’re missing a few key elements.

> For example : Will the role have defined objectives? Will these be included in the job description? When and how will performance be reviewed? Which skills are required to successfully meet expectations? Which ones are minimum requirements?

I feel like because it’s a new role, we tend to forget that it requires the same as any other full-time job of the company, meaning :

- A clear, step-by-step HR process Define the full lifecycle of the role : identification, onboarding plan, performance reviews, check-ins, and long-term development processes.
- Minimum requirements for the ideal candidate profile Specify the essential qualifications : experience level, technical skills, soft skills, certifications, or any prerequisites needed to perform the job effectively.
- A skills reference framework for the role Create a detailed list of all competencies expected in this position, both technical and behavioral, and describe what level is expected for each skill.
- A gap assessment between existing and target skills Evaluate the current team member(s) against the role’s required competencies to identify strengths, development areas, and training needs.
- A plan to promote and support these new roles (career path, training, etc.) Outline how the role fits into the broader organization : potential career progression, internal mobility opportunities, available training programs, and communication around the importance of this new function.
👉 What you have to remember is that it is MANDATORY to work with HR team for this framing phase - otherwise it won’t work.

## Preparing the launch

Now.. have you prepared with whom they’ll work? Report issues? In which tools? At which frequency? Will they be trained?

All these topics are part of what I call the Data Stewardship Playbook .

## Cool, what’s in it?

Wait, wait, before we dive in. The first question is : who is it for?

Well, it’s for Data Owners. You need to go see them with a full playbook to facilitate their life, and by that I mean facilitating the way they onboard their Data Stewards.

The playbook should cover :

- The vision of Data Governance for your organization That’s about explaining the strategy in simple words, no jargon.
- The operating model with the different roles Here you focus and explain where specifically are the Data Stewards, with whom they’ll work / report to.
- The deployment planning Outline how the governance framework will be rolled out : key phases, timelines, priorities, and what’s expected from each stakeholder. You should also precise if all domains will be launched together or by waves.
- The toolkit (this is super important 😅) Present the practical tasks Data Stewards will perform and how : actions, templates, dashboards, glossaries, workflows, etc. Show how each tool simplifies their work and supports consistent governance across the organization.
