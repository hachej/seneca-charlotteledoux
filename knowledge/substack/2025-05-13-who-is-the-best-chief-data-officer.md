## 🎮 What’s the game about ?

In this interactive game, you take on the role of a Chief Data Officer at a fictional company. Your goal : balance innovation with compliance, win support across departments, manage data risks, and prove the value of data to the business.

[Play](https://www.whoisthebestcdo.com/)

You’ll face choices like :

- How to handle a data quality or data breach crisis
- Prioritizing between AI initiatives and compliance projects
- Convincing the board to invest in data & AI
- Navigating organizational politics and resource constraints
> 👉 All this happens by selecting an answer to each email received in your inbox .

In the game, the impact of your choices are represented by 2 key indicators :

📊 Data quality : Improving your data quality will avoid you “data crisis” on regulation, breach or quality . They are represented through urgent emails that must be answered in less than 20 seconds.

⭐️ Reputation : Increasing your reputation will allow you to get more budget and to be consulted on strategic orientations . It simulates the fact that people are convinced by the value of using data.

You have to manage these indicators for the best, but the ultimate goal is to increase the company’s profit .

> Remember that one wrong decision could cost the company millions .

## How to win…

Success in the game isn’t about chasing one goal : it’s about finding the right balance . To become the best CDO, you'll need to weigh innovation against risk, short-term wins against long-term strategy, and technology against people and culture.

Make too many bold moves and you risk losing trust and reputation ; play it too safe and you’ll miss opportunities .

The best CDO is the one who brings it all together.

> 🔍 Hint : don’t forget that you can WAIT before answering non-urgent emails, as long as you have less than 7 in your inbox. Waiting can give you time to do better choices, or get more budget…
