## The biggest challenge is…

This one is not going to be a surprise. At least it was not for me, but I guess it’s not fair as I’ve spent the last year working on Data Governance with around 10 different companies 😅

… 🥁 getting the business to care and change habits for 61% of you !

> The other top challenges are : 45% for unclear ownership and roles 42% for data quality

I think we can now all stop pretending the failure comes from the tool or the platform… Managing the change, raising awareness and adoption are long and not really sexy, but once again that is our job.

[Subscribe now](https://thedatagovernanceplaybook.substack.com/subscribe?)

Someone said it plainly when I asked what you wish worked better in your data governance program :

“ Openness from people and willingness to work with the new behavior instead of sticking to old stuff. ”

Ouch. But don’t forget that willingness on changing a behavior comes first with recognizing the value of the work provided the old way !

## Still an IT stepchild

Let’s see where Data Governance reports in 2026 :

- 43% report under IT/CTO. Only 26% under a CDO
- The rest scattered across business units, compliance, finance.
When governance lives in IT, the business reads it as an IT project. Which means someone else’s job ! That’s how you get the “won’t change habits” problem.

When governance lives in IT, it’s also harder to get proper sponsorship . On this topic, the results are literally a theatre :

- Only 25% have an active, visible executive sponsor. 44% say “yes on paper, quiet in practice,” and 31% have none/unclear . The sponsor gap is also the mechanism behind the change management challenge.
In the survey I had left some text fields to collect qualitative insights. Guess what :

> “Ownership” is the second most repeated word after “Sponsorship” 🤯

Somebody wrote clearly what they need on this topic :

“End-to-end process ownership and accountability linked to data objectives”.

## The budget squeeze

It looks like Data Governance is expected to deliver on goodwill :

- 42% have no dedicated budget at all.
- 28% flat, 24% growing, 5% shrinking.
- Meanwhile proving the value / getting budget is also part of top challenges at 40%.
I think the way out isn’t a bigger ask, it’s picking one visible win that a sponsor can fully support . And you know [I mean a business sponsor](https://thedatagovernanceplaybook.substack.com/p/5-ideas-to-get-data-governance-sponsors) !

What I’m happy to see is that your top priority for the year at 37% is to roll out or fix the operating model (roles, domains). Fixing roles usually start with getting sponsors, then having clear data owners who will name data stewards.

---

## The tooling paradox

Buying the platform didn’t fix the problem, because the problem was never the platform, haha !

- A majority have tooling : 33% have a dedicated governance/catalog platform , another 23% run a mix of specific tools.
- 14% have documents and spreadsheets as tooling for now.
- Yet data quality is still a top 3 challenge at 42%.
Well that’s funny, [the data catalog doesn’t create ownership magically](https://lnkd.in/p/exb69fZa) ! And ownership is what fixes quality, so... I’ve seen many teams buy a tool to avoid the harder change management work and then still have the change management work, minus the budget 😩

## The AI Governance gap

AI governance is mostly talk, and the [AI Act’s literacy obligation](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/4/) is a deadline almost nobody’s ready for.

- AI governance “in progress” for 50% but only 19% have a framework actually running. 13% haven’t started.
- AI Act literacy obligation : only 18% have trained their people. 25% rolling out, and the rest is under discussion or not even aware of it. So roughly 6 in 10 aren’t really moving.
> The AI Act’s Article 4 literacy requirement has applied since February 2025 and 18% have never heard of it. What? Yes, the EU AI Act requires organizations to ensure AI literacy among staff who use AI .

The survey also says that generic content won’t move it, people want content that fits their business. What people actually want :

“Training in partnership with the HR Learning & Development team, actively sponsored by exco.” That’s it.
