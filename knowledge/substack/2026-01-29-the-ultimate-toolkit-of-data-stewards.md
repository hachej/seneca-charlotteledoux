## How to build it

Say you have worked hard on the [onboarding of your Data Stewards](https://thedatagovernanceplaybook.substack.com/p/onboarding-your-data-stewards). You have a clear Data Governance organisation, with framed roles, skills and RACI.

👉 Wait, no? Then [grab my advanced templates](https://charlotteledoux.notion.site/Data-Governance-Advanced-Templates-2e0c19a5aae88058bd38d42243fe19ab) !

What you need now, is to add to your Data Stewardship Playbook a TOOLKIT .

The toolkit will present the practical tasks of Data Stewards : actions, templates, dashboards, glossaries, workflows, etc. It will show also how each tool simplifies their work and supports consistent governance across the organization.

What I strongly recommend to build it efficiently is :

- Prepare a first version of the toolkit
- Find 3-4 Data Stewards volunteer to help you
- Organize a workshop with these 3-4 Data Stewards to challenge the toolkit you prepared
- Update the content with their feedback / create new parts if missing
The idea is to co-create the document with Data Stewards and ensure that they participate and take ownership of it. This will prevent future rejections.

## What’s in it

The toolkit is basically a procedural guide to enable data stewards to work independently in their tasks. It should be easy to understand !

Usually, I build it by following the 8 steps for deploying a data domain :

1️⃣ Scope the subdomain

Map out the data sources, flows, and key processes within your domain.

> ✏️ Deliverable : domain mapping or simplified diagram

2️⃣ Define the vision

Identify the key business questions to solve and prioritize them by impact.

> ✏️ Deliverable : list of key questions and prioritization table

3️⃣ Identification of critical data assets

Pinpoint the most critical data assets linked to the important business questions and their associated pain points.

> ✏️ Deliverable : “domain heatmap” matrix

4️⃣ Build the business glossary

Create standardized business terms with definitions and business rules linked to each critical data asset.

> ✏️ Deliverable : glossary sheets for each critical element

5️⃣ Measure data quality

Define quality rules for these assets, KPIs, and thresholds, then build quality dashboards.

> ✏️ Deliverable : quality indicator monitoring dashboard

6️⃣ Drive remediation plans

Analyze root causes of issues and track corrective actions to resolution.

> ✏️ Deliverable : action plan with responsible person, deadline, priority

7️⃣ Populate the data catalogue

Document and publish metadata, linking business terms to technical assets.

> ✏️ Deliverable : complete documentation available in the data catalog

8️⃣ Animate data governance and track performance

Run domain committees and monitor quality and overall progress of data governance deployment.

> ✏️ Deliverable : committee report and performance dashboard

For each of these steps, you should also explain how it will happen, in which process, with which tool and the estimated workload.

👉 You also must mentioned who will contribute to the task . Here for the scoping, the IT manager or IT Data Steward should help to list sources and flows.

Note : you should adapt these steps to your organization, for example you could have a “Certify data assets” step !

## What, Data Stewards are not using AI?

Sure they are ! You should clarify at each step which tool they will use and how they will use it (in which process?).

The key for Data Stewards is to use AI as an accelerator , but they will do the validation, add business context, and work on stakeholder alignment.

## The diffusion

This is actually the most important part. You should spend 80% of your time on the distribution of the toolkit and only 20% on the creation.

Let’s diffuse it at scale through :

## 🧑‍🏫 Structured onboarding sessions

- Host a webinar with all Data Stewards to present the final toolkit and walk through each of the 8 deployment steps with practical examples
- Run dedicated training sessions for each Data Steward (Business and IT) with hands-on demos of tools (OpenMetadata, quality dashboards) - do this on a regular basis to train new data stewards
## 🗃️ Centralized documentation hub

- Create a shared repository (SharePoint, Confluence, or intranet) containing : The toolkit PowerPoint
- Templates for each deliverable (domain vision, glossary, quality rules, remediation plan)
- Video tutorials and FAQs
- Links to tools and dashboards
- A folder for each domain to drop their deliverables
## 🪩 Community animation

- Launch the “Data” community with specific sessions to : Share best practices between Data Stewards
- Celebrate quick wins and success stories
- Create peer-to-peer support and mentoring
- Reward them with unique opportunities (tickets to conferences, badges to share on LinkedIn, etc.)
- Push weekly Teams message with the “hot” links of the repository (what people consult the most)
## 📣 Regular touchpoints

- Internal committee domain Frequency : Monthly Objective : Track the progress of initiatives within a specific data domain (quality improvements, monitoring, remediation plans) Participants : Data Domain Owner / Business & IT Data Stewards / Data Governance Manager
- Cross-domain committee Frequency : Every 2 months Objective : Align transversal initiatives across all data domains and arbitrate on shared rules, definitions, and concepts Participants : Data Domain Owners (all domains) / CDO / Data Governance Manager / IT Director
